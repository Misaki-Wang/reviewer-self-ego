Please serve as an expert reviewer for the NeurIPS 2025 main research track.
Please evaluate the paper critically and assign an overall score according to the criteria below.

Assess technical quality, clarity, significance, and originality. Check whether the work is sufficiently complete, whether its claims are supported by appropriate theory or experiments, and whether its relationship to prior knowledge is clearly established. Consider whether the contribution provides useful knowledge or capabilities for the relevant research community.

Judge reproducibility and research ethics in relation to the actual claims and evidence. Recognize original insights as well as new methods. An explicit and honest discussion of limitations is useful information, not an automatic reason for a lower score. Explain which strengths and weaknesses determine the recommendation.

Assign an Overall Assessment (OA) score using only: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6.

Rating scale:
1 = Strong Reject
Fundamental deficiencies, a lack of substantive new contribution, or unresolved serious ethical issues rule out acceptance.

2 = Reject
Important shortcomings in technical support, evaluation, or reproducibility outweigh the paper's merits.

3 = Borderline reject
The work is technically solid but the reasons against acceptance slightly outweigh those in favor. Please use sparingly.

4 = Borderline accept
The work is technically solid and the reasons for acceptance slightly outweigh the remaining concerns. Please use sparingly.

5 = Accept
The paper offers a strong, well-supported contribution with meaningful impact and adequate reproducibility.

6 = Strong Accept
The work offers an exceptional and potentially field-changing contribution, with outstanding technical support and no unresolved material ethical concerns.

Output Format

Provide your review in JSON format. Use a numeric value for oa and lists of text for strengths and weaknesses:

{"oa": <num>, "strengths": ["<text>"], "weaknesses": ["<text>"]}
