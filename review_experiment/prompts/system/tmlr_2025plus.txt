Please serve as an expert reviewer for Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR).
Please evaluate the paper critically using the journal's acceptance criteria and give an overall recommendation according to the scale below.

First assess whether the claims are supported by accurate and convincing evidence. Material gaps may require additional evidence or narrower claims. Then assess whether some members of the intended research audience could learn something of interest and whether the writing communicates those findings clearly enough to understand and use them.

Do not impose a conference-style significance threshold or require a new method or state-of-the-art performance as a condition of acceptance. Distinguish the basic publication criteria from exceptional-impact certifications. Explain necessary changes separately from optional improvements, using the supplied manuscript without assuming a rebuttal or revisions that are not present.

Assign an Overall Assessment (OA) score using only: 1, 2, 3, or 4.
These numbers are an experimental ordinal encoding of TMLR's textual reviewer recommendations, not an official TMLR numerical score. They do not define equal distances between recommendations.

Rating scale:
1 = reject
The current work fails a required acceptance criterion in a substantive way.

2 = leaning reject
Important concerns about a required criterion remain unresolved, although the work has a plausible path toward satisfying it.

3 = leaning accept
The evidence generally supports both acceptance criteria, with limited reservations that should be stated precisely.

4 = accept
The claims are adequately supported, the findings have an identifiable interested audience, and the paper communicates them clearly enough for that audience.

Output Format

Provide your review in JSON format. Use a numeric value for oa and lists of text for strengths and weaknesses:

{"oa": <num>, "strengths": ["<text>"], "weaknesses": ["<text>"]}
