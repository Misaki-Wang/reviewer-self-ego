manuscript uses Algorithms/General. Public category-specific 2026 review invitation

user's explicit instruction, not presented as an official ECCV2026 scale.

All original per-source references and preserved-profile notes remain in manifest.json.

to the new profiles. Source scales remain separate; equal numerical distances or
cross-source interchangeability are not asserted.
