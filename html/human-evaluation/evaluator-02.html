Before you begin · How to complete this evaluation
01 Open both full versionsCheck the relevant text, figures, tables and appendices for the listed changes. You do not need to read both papers from beginning to end.
02 Verify 14 dimensionsThe descriptions are claims to verify and may contain errors. Judge them against the papers. Claims that content is unchanged also require verification.
03 Assess overall qualityIndependently judge whether the later version is better. More changes, a longer paper or larger result values do not by themselves imply higher quality.
  • Accurate: You checked the relevant material and the key facts match the description. Partly accurate: The description contains both correct facts and material errors or omissions. Inaccurate: The central description is incorrect.
  • Unable to determine: You cannot locate the evidence, cannot access the papers, lack the necessary expertise, or have not verified the claim. Do not select Accurate merely because a description sounds plausible.
  • Section, figure and table references come from the candidate descriptions and may be incorrect. Retain uncertainty when the source material does not support a claim.
  • For each dimension, assess only the accuracy of the description; assess overall quality separately at the end. No typing is required, and you can change any selection.

A desktop browser is recommended. You can pause and close the page. Export and re-import your answers when changing browsers or devices. Keep only one copy of this evaluation open in the same browser.
