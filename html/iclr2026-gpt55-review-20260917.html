Limitations and provenance

  • Historical per-attempt effective client base instructions and full client metadata equality are not attested.
  • Catalog warning snapshots do not establish which historical client metadata was effective for each attempt.
  • Read-command patterns are descriptive evidence, not proof of comprehension or complete page coverage.
  • No cross-account assistance is supported by this adapter; a separate validated proof is required.
  • Execution-attempt counts are not provider request counts; unreported internal retries remain unknown.
  • Recorded runtime paths are checked through invocation metadata and exact fingerprints, not local executable resolution or a claim of historical binary-content attestation.
  • Local /proc observations are limited to the audit host PID namespace. Without an original host/boot/PID-namespace binding they do not prove historical remote owner absence; terminal records and shared output locks are separate evidence.
  • The unchanged original canonical dataset retains its fatal read/environment finding; this certificate targets only the explicit derived accepted view.
  • The superseded native-completed result and all of its usage remain in physical history.
  • Original attempt 6909 retains its separate content-quality caution; it is not replaced.
  • This is a model-selected major-improvement cohort, not a representative sample of all ICLR submissions.
  • There is one review per version and condition. Conditions are reported separately; no significance tests, confidence intervals, causal claims, or cross-model comparison are made.
  • The 255 frozen comparison pairs share some papers or endpoints. Version reviews are reused for applicable pairs, not counted as new calls.
  • All-attempt totals include failed, superseded and recovery work. Missing usage and unreported provider retries remain unknown.
  • Exact model responses may quote their assigned manuscript file path. Only that receipt-bound input citation is retained; unrelated private paths and credentials remain excluded. Such text is review data and is never executed.
  • Original attempt 6909d3288a9c4a9285596b11cbd9c59f retains its content-quality caution; no replacement was selected for it.
