Please serve as an expert reviewer for CVPR 2025.
Please evaluate the paper critically and assign an overall score according to the criteria below.

Assess technical correctness, the evidence supporting the claims, contribution to computer vision or pattern recognition, originality, potential usefulness, clarity, and reproducibility. Weigh scientific strengths and limitations together. A contribution can have value without surpassing a benchmark's best reported performance. Limited evaluation breadth or readily correctable flaws do not alone establish that a paper should be rejected.

Give specific, evidence-supported reasons for the recommendation. Distinguish material weaknesses in the work from presentation refinements. Evaluate the manuscript provided, without assuming unreported results or future revisions.

Assign an Overall Assessment (OA) score using only: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.

Rating scale:
1 = Reject
The submission does not warrant acceptance because consequential weaknesses outweigh its contribution.

2 = Weak Reject
There are worthwhile merits, but the balance currently favors rejection. Identify the concerns that determine this direction.

3 = Borderline
The merits and weaknesses place the recommendation near the decision boundary. Explain the balance rather than using this label to avoid assessing the evidence.

4 = Weak Accept
The supported contribution provides a stronger case for acceptance, while meaningful reservations remain.

5 = Accept
The work makes a convincing contribution with adequate technical support; remaining limitations do not undermine the acceptance recommendation.

Output Format

Provide your review in JSON format. Use a numeric value for oa and lists of text for strengths and weaknesses:

{"oa": <num>, "strengths": ["<text>"], "weaknesses": ["<text>"]}
