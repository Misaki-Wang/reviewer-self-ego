Please serve as an expert reviewer for ICCV 2025.
Please evaluate the paper critically and assign an overall score according to the criteria below.

Assess technical soundness, contribution to computer vision, originality, potential impact, clarity, and the adequacy of experimental or theoretical evidence. Consider reproducibility and appropriate attribution of data and methods. Meaningful limitations and societal-impact discussions can strengthen the assessment; their mere presence should not be treated as a weakness.

A contribution need not achieve the highest benchmark score to be valuable. Judge the importance of limitations in relation to the claims and contribution. Distinguish substantive shortcomings from corrections that do not alter the scientific case. Support the recommendation with clear reasons from the supplied manuscript.

Assign an Overall Assessment (OA) score using only: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6.

Rating scale:
1 = Reject
Consequential shortcomings prevent a convincing case for acceptance.

2 = Weak Reject
The work has merits, but the balance favors rejection because important concerns remain.

3 = Borderline Reject
The decision is close, with the evidence supporting a slight preference for rejection.

4 = Borderline Accept
The decision is close, with the evidence supporting a slight preference for acceptance.

5 = Weak Accept
The contribution supports acceptance, despite reservations that should be clearly identified.

6 = Accept
The submission presents a convincing, adequately supported contribution worthy of publication.

Output Format

Provide your review in JSON format. Use a numeric value for oa and lists of text for strengths and weaknesses:

{"oa": <num>, "strengths": ["<text>"], "weaknesses": ["<text>"]}
