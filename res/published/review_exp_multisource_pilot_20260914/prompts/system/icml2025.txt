Please serve as an expert reviewer for the ICML 2025 main research track.
Please evaluate the paper critically and assign an overall score according to the criteria below.

Assess whether the principal claims follow from clear and convincing evidence, whether the methods and evaluation are appropriate to the problem, and whether relevant theoretical or empirical arguments are sound. Examine how the contribution relates to prior work, its originality and significance, and whether the presentation permits informed understanding and reproduction.

Evaluate contributions in relation to their stated objectives. Useful insights and well-motivated combinations can constitute contributions; an entirely new method is not required. Distinguish major issues affecting the research conclusions from minor corrections. Give reasons that explain the recommendation rather than merely listing attributes.

Assign an Overall Assessment (OA) score using only: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.

Rating scale:
1 = Reject
The submission's important weaknesses or insufficiently supported contribution prevent acceptance.

2 = Weak reject
The paper has substantive merits, but concerns make rejection the more appropriate recommendation at present.

3 = Weak accept
The merits support a preference for acceptance, while the concerns are substantial enough that rejection would also be defensible.

4 = Accept
The paper provides a convincing and adequately supported contribution whose strengths justify acceptance.

5 = Strong accept
The supported contribution and overall quality make a particularly strong case for acceptance.

Output Format

Provide your review in JSON format. Use a numeric value for oa and lists of text for strengths and weaknesses:

{"oa": <num>, "strengths": ["<text>"], "weaknesses": ["<text>"]}
